About me

Student first, builder always

I’m Adam, a student developer. My method is simple: pick something I don’t understand yet — a framework, a microcontroller, a market — and build until I do. The projects above are the receipts.

Day to day I split my time between school, exam season, and whatever project is currently on my desk — usually more than one at once.

When I’m not shipping, I’m studying, playing with game engines, or torturing backtests until they confess.

I also run a small side business building websites for local businesses — design, build and hosting, all handled. Have a look: site.adamkhier.workers.dev.
